Privacy Policy

Di Balik Merdeka Publisher · last updated 24 November 2026

1. Summary

The app is a private publishing tool. It does not collect, store or share personal data about anyone. It has no public users, no sign-up, no analytics and no tracking.

2. What the app accesses

To publish videos to accounts we own, the app is authorised through each platform's official login flow. That authorisation grants access tokens limited to the permissions shown on the consent screen:

  • TikTok: user.info.basic, video.upload, video.publish
  • YouTube: youtube.upload, youtube
  • Instagram: instagram_basic, instagram_content_publish, pages_show_list, pages_read_engagement, business_management

These permissions are used only to upload our own videos and to read back the identifier of a published post. The app does not read messages, followers, analytics or any other user's data.

3. What the app stores

Access and refresh tokens are stored in a single file on the operator's own computer, readable only by that user (file mode 600). They are never sent anywhere except back to the platform that issued them. A local log records which episode was published to which platform, so the same video is not posted twice.

We do not operate a server, database or cloud storage for this app.

4. Sharing

No data is sold, rented or shared with third parties. The only outbound connections are to the official APIs of TikTok, Google/YouTube and Meta/Instagram.

5. Retention and deletion

Tokens remain on the operator's machine until they expire or are deleted. Revoking access from the platform's own settings (TikTok → Settings → Security → Manage app permissions; Google Account → Security → Third-party access; Meta → Apps and websites) immediately invalidates them. Deleting the local token file removes them from the machine entirely.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children and processes no data about them.

7. Changes

Any change to this policy will be published at this URL with an updated date.
